Follow the Roman Catholic Mass

A simple, step-by-step guide to what is happening, what the priest says, what the congregation says, and when to stand, sit, kneel, bow, or make the Sign of the Cross.

Before Mass Begins

Entering the Church and Preparing
Genuflect or bow Sit or kneel
If the tabernacle containing the Blessed Sacrament is visible, genuflect toward it before entering your pew. If you are physically unable to genuflect, make a reverent bow.
Enter quietly. You may kneel or sit for private prayer. Silence your phone and prepare your heart for Mass.

1. Introductory Rites

Entrance Procession
Stand
What happensThe entrance hymn begins and the priest, deacon, and ministers process toward the altar.
Stand when the people around you stand, normally as the entrance procession begins.
Sign of the Cross
Remain standing Make the Sign of the Cross
PriestThe priest begins in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.
CongregationAmen.
Liturgical Greeting
Remain standing
PriestThe priest gives one of the approved greetings, usually ending with “The Lord be with you.”
CongregationAnd with your spirit.
Penitential Act — Asking Forgiveness
Remain standing
PriestThe priest invites everyone to acknowledge their sins and prepare to celebrate the sacred mysteries.
One of several approved forms may be used. Your parish may pray the Confiteor beginning “I confess to almighty God…,” use short invocations, or use the blessing and sprinkling of water on certain Sundays.
When the Confiteor is used, strike your breast three times at the words “through my fault, through my fault, through my most grievous fault.”
Congregation — Kyrie Lord, have mercy.
Christ, have mercy.
Lord, have mercy.
PriestThe priest concludes with a prayer asking God to show mercy and lead us to everlasting life.
CongregationAmen.

The Penitential Act is not the Sacrament of Penance and does not replace sacramental Confession for mortal sin.

Gloria — “Glory to God”
Remain standing
Priest, cantor, or choirThe Gloria begins.
Sing or recite the Gloria with the congregation, using the words or musical setting in the parish missal or hymn book.

The Gloria is normally used on Sundays outside Advent and Lent, and on solemnities and feasts.

Collect — Opening Prayer
Remain standing
Priest“Let us pray.” After a brief silence, the priest says the prayer appointed for that day.
CongregationAmen.
Sit after the prayer is finished.

2. Liturgy of the Word

First Reading
Sit
ReaderThe reader proclaims the first Scripture reading, then says, “The word of the Lord.”
CongregationThanks be to God.
Responsorial Psalm
Remain seated
Cantor or readerThe cantor or reader announces and leads the Psalm response.
CongregationRepeat the Psalm response announced for that day.
Second Reading
Remain seated
ReaderOn Sundays and solemnities, a second Scripture reading is normally proclaimed, ending with “The word of the Lord.”
CongregationThanks be to God.
Gospel Acclamation
Stand
Cantor or choirThe Alleluia and verse are sung. During Lent, another Gospel acclamation is used instead of Alleluia.
CongregationSing the Alleluia or the response led by the cantor.
Gospel
Remain standing
Priest or deacon“The Lord be with you.”
CongregationAnd with your spirit.
Priest or deacon“A reading from the holy Gospel according to…”
CongregationGlory to you, O Lord.
Make a small cross with your thumb on your forehead, lips, and heart: may the Gospel be in your mind, on your lips, and in your heart.
After the GospelThe priest or deacon says, “The Gospel of the Lord.”
CongregationPraise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.
Homily
Sit
Priest or deaconThe homily explains the Scriptures and applies them to Christian faith and life.
Profession of Faith — Nicene Creed
Stand
Bow deeply during the words about the Son of God becoming incarnate of the Virgin Mary and becoming man.
Congregation I believe in one God, the Father almighty, maker of heaven and earth, of all things visible and invisible.
I believe in one Lord Jesus Christ, the Only Begotten Son of God, born of the Father before all ages. God from God, Light from Light, true God from true God, begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father; through him all things were made. For us men and for our salvation he came down from heaven, and by the Holy Spirit was incarnate of the Virgin Mary, and became man.
For our sake he was crucified under Pontius Pilate, he suffered death and was buried, and rose again on the third day in accordance with the Scriptures. He ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father. He will come again in glory to judge the living and the dead and his kingdom will have no end.
I believe in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the giver of life, who proceeds from the Father and the Son, who with the Father and the Son is adored and glorified, who has spoken through the prophets.
I believe in one, holy, catholic and apostolic Church. I confess one Baptism for the forgiveness of sins and I look forward to the resurrection of the dead and the life of the world to come. Amen.

The Apostles’ Creed may be used on some occasions instead.

Universal Prayer — Prayer of the Faithful
Remain standing
Reader or deaconIntentions are offered for the Church, the world, those in need, and the local community.
CongregationUse the response announced by the reader, commonly: Lord, hear our prayer.
Sit after the priest’s concluding prayer.

3. Liturgy of the Eucharist

Preparation of the Altar and Gifts
Sit
What happensThe altar is prepared. Bread and wine are brought forward. A collection may be taken.
The priest’s prayers at the altar may be said quietly or aloud. If they are said aloud, the congregation may respond, “Blessed be God for ever.”
Invitation to Prayer Over the Offerings
Stand
PriestThe priest invites the people to pray that the sacrifice may be acceptable to God.
Congregation May the Lord accept the sacrifice at your hands for the praise and glory of his name, for our good and the good of all his holy Church.
PriestThe priest says the Prayer over the Offerings.
CongregationAmen.
Preface Dialogue
Remain standing
PriestThe Lord be with you.
CongregationAnd with your spirit.
PriestLift up your hearts.
CongregationWe lift them up to the Lord.
PriestLet us give thanks to the Lord our God.
CongregationIt is right and just.
Holy, Holy, Holy — Sanctus
Remain standing while singing
Congregation Holy, Holy, Holy Lord God of hosts.
Heaven and earth are full of your glory.
Hosanna in the highest.
Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord.
Hosanna in the highest.
Kneel immediately after the Holy, Holy, Holy, following the local custom and the congregation.
Eucharistic Prayer and Consecration
Kneel
PriestThe priest prays the Eucharistic Prayer. The words vary according to the Eucharistic Prayer chosen.
Listen prayerfully. At the consecration, the priest shows the consecrated Host and then the Chalice. You may look toward the Lord in adoration and silently pray, “My Lord and my God.”
PriestThe priest announces the mystery of faith.
Congregation — one of these is used We proclaim your Death, O Lord, and profess your Resurrection until you come again.

or

When we eat this Bread and drink this Cup, we proclaim your Death, O Lord, until you come again.

or

Save us, Savior of the world, for by your Cross and Resurrection you have set us free.
Great Amen
Remain kneeling
PriestThe Eucharistic Prayer ends with the doxology as the Host and Chalice are raised.
CongregationAmen.
Stand after the Great Amen.

4. Communion Rite

The Lord’s Prayer — Our Father
Stand
PriestThe priest introduces the prayer taught by Jesus.
Congregation Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come; thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.
PriestThe priest continues with the prayer for deliverance from evil.
Congregation For the kingdom, the power and the glory are yours now and for ever.
Sign of Peace
Remain standing
PriestThe peace of the Lord be with you always.
CongregationAnd with your spirit.
When invited, offer a simple sign of peace to the people immediately around you, such as a handshake, nod, or small bow, according to local custom.
Lamb of God — Agnus Dei
Stand, then follow local posture
Congregation Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us.
Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, have mercy on us.
Lamb of God, you take away the sins of the world, grant us peace.
In many U.S. parishes, the congregation kneels after the Lamb of God. Follow the established practice of your diocese and parish.
Invitation to Holy Communion
Usually kneeling
PriestThe priest shows the consecrated Host and invites the faithful to the supper of the Lamb.
Priest and Congregation together Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof, but only say the word and my soul shall be healed.
Receiving Holy Communion
Stand for the procession Bow your head
Join the Communion procession only if properly disposed to receive. As the person immediately before you receives, bow your head reverently.
MinisterThe Body of Christ.
You respond clearlyAmen.
If receiving from the ChaliceThe Blood of Christ.
You respond clearlyAmen.
Holy Communion is normally received standing in the United States, either on the tongue or in the hand. A communicant who chooses to kneel is not to be denied Communion for that reason.
Catholics conscious of mortal sin should ordinarily receive sacramental absolution before receiving Holy Communion. Non-Catholics and Catholics not receiving may remain in the pew in prayer; local customs about approaching for a blessing vary.
Prayer After Receiving Communion
Sit or kneel
Return to your place and pray quietly in thanksgiving. Follow your parish’s custom about sitting or kneeling while Communion continues.
Prayer After Communion
Stand
Priest“Let us pray.” The priest says the Prayer after Communion appointed for that day.
CongregationAmen.

5. Concluding Rites

Announcements, Greeting, and Blessing
Stand
PriestThe Lord be with you.
CongregationAnd with your spirit.
Bow your head if the deacon or priest invites you to do so for a solemn blessing.
PriestThe priest blesses the congregation in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.
CongregationAmen.
Dismissal
Remain standing
Deacon or priestOne of the approved dismissals is used, such as “Go forth, the Mass is ended.”
CongregationThanks be to God.
Remain until the priest and ministers have reverenced the altar and the procession begins. Join the closing hymn when one is sung.
Leaving the Church
Genuflect or bow
Before leaving your pew, genuflect toward the tabernacle if the Blessed Sacrament is reserved there. If unable to genuflect, make a reverent bow.
